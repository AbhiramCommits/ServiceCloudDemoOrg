# Requirements Traceability — Northwind Outfitters

Every named pain from `docs/discovery-brief.md` mapped to a business requirement, the Salesforce
capability that satisfies it, the concrete artifact(s) in this repo, and the metric movement
produced by `sim/` and recorded in `results/metrics.json`.

> Paths under `force-app/` are deployable SFDX metadata. Paths under `sim/` are the local harness
> that mirrors the same business rules so results are reproducible without an org. Every **Metric
> Moved** value below is a real number from `results/metrics.json` (or, for the assistant, from
> `results/assistant_eval.json`), regenerated by `make all`.

| Pain ID | Business Requirement | Salesforce Capability | Where It Lives in This Repo | Metric Moved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | One durable record per customer request, joined to the customer's order and visible to the whole team instead of the shared inbox plus spreadsheet. | Service Cloud custom object `Support_Request__c` with a related-order lookup, a record-triggered lifecycle, and a Lightning record page. | `force-app/main/default/objects/Support_Request__c/`, `force-app/main/default/flexipages/Support_Console_Record_Page.flexipage-meta.xml`, `force-app/main/default/triggers/SupportRequestTrigger.trigger`, `sim/run_simulation.py` | Human-handled cases per agent per day **15.87 → 6.83** and backlog age **33.81 h → 3.10 h** once duplicate re-typing/searching leaves the process (`metrics.cases_per_agent_per_day`, `metrics.backlog_age_hours`). |
| P2 | Score every request by customer lifetime value, channel and topic; assign a priority-based SLA; and route work so high-value requests never queue behind low-value ones. | Record-triggered Flow triage plus `PriorityRouter` Apex scoring, the SLA matrix, least-loaded owner assignment, and a scheduled escalation path. | `force-app/main/default/flows/Support_Request_Triage.flow-meta.xml`, `force-app/main/default/classes/PriorityRouter.cls`, `force-app/main/default/flows/Case_Escalation_Alert.flow-meta.xml`, `sim/priority_router.py` | SLA compliance **43.15% → 97.30%**, SLA breaches **2,274 → 108 per month**, first-response p90 **1,293.65 → 241.24 min** (`metrics.sla_compliance_pct`, `metrics.first_response_minutes_p90`, `diagnostics.sla_breaches`). |
| P3 | Deflect the six recurring questions through self-service and escalate anything the assistant cannot ground. | Agentforce-style deflection agent with six invocable Apex actions, deflection articles, and an intent router with a confidence threshold. | `force-app/main/default/classes/DeflectionAgentActions.cls`, `agentforce/deflection-agent.md`, `force-app/main/default/objects/Deflection_Article__c/`, `sim/assistant.py`, `data/intent_eval.csv` | Deflection rate **0.00% → 56.95%** (**2,278** requests/month kept off the queue) releasing **592.58 agent-hours per month** (`metrics.deflection_rate_pct`, `diagnostics.deflected_requests`, `diagnostics.agent_hours_freed_per_month`); assistant accuracy **97.86%**, escalation precision **90.48%** (`results/assistant_eval.json`). |

| P5 | Determine warranty / entitlement eligibility automatically from the customer's order history. | `WarrantyService` Apex plus the `Warranty_Eligibility_Check` Flow over the order data model. | `force-app/main/default/classes/WarrantyService.cls`, `force-app/main/default/flows/Warranty_Eligibility_Check.flow-meta.xml`, `force-app/main/default/objects/Order_Record__c/`, `sim/warranty.py` | Removes the manual store-admin lookup from every warranty/return case, contributing to the **−57.0%** human-handled load and the first-response mean of **80.54 min**; warranty verdicts are now grounded in order history rather than guessed (`metrics.cases_per_agent_per_day`, `metrics.first_response_minutes_mean`). |

## Notes

- All metric values above are generated by `python -m sim.run_simulation --out results/` and
  `python -m sim.eval_assistant --out results/`, and are committed under `results/`.
- The dataset is synthetic but Northwind-scale (4,000 requests/month, 12 agents, 30 days). See
  `docs/value-model.md` for the annualized business value and `docs/discovery-brief.md` for the
  customer-stated baselines these movements are measured against.
